# Teaching standards (the StudyPipe "best learning standard")

There is no single official international standard. These are the research-backed methods
used in teacher training worldwide. Every lesson applies all of them.

## A. Planning
1. **Backward design (Wiggins & McTighe, Understanding by Design).** Start from what the student
   must be able to DO, decide how that is checked, then decide what to teach.
2. **Bloom's taxonomy (revised).** Remember → Understand → Apply → Analyse → Evaluate → Create.
   Objectives and questions must go beyond "remember"; WAEC tests apply/analyse.
3. **Prerequisites first.** Name what must already be known; give a short refresher.

## B. Lesson structure
4. **Gagné's Nine Events:** gain attention → state objectives → recall prior knowledge → present
   content → guide learning → practice → feedback → assess → help retention and transfer.
5. **5E model (science):** Engage → Explore → Explain → Elaborate → Evaluate.
   The 3D practical is the *Explore* phase: the student acts before being told.
6. **Gradual release:** I do (worked example) → We do (guided, with hints) → You do (alone).

## C. Explaining
7. **Cognitive load theory.** Small chunks, one idea at a time, nothing extra on screen.
8. **Mayer's multimedia principles.** Words + pictures; narration with visuals rather than
   on-screen paragraphs; signal what to look at; remove decoration that doesn't teach;
   keep labels next to what they label.
9. **Concrete → Representational → Abstract.** Real object/event → diagram/model → symbols/formula.
10. **Worked examples** with every step's reason, before independent problems.
11. **Misconceptions** stated, shown wrong (ideally in a scene), and replaced with the correct model.
12. **Familiar context.** Nigerian everyday examples make new ideas stick.

## D. Making it stick
13. **Retrieval practice:** frequent short recall questions, not re-reading.
14. **Spaced practice:** end with 2–3 questions from earlier related topics.
15. **Interleaving:** mix question types so students must choose the method.
16. **Formative assessment with feedback:** checks inside the lesson; every answer option
    has an explanation, especially wrong ones.

## E. Inclusion
17. **Universal Design for Learning:** every key idea in at least two forms (visual + audio/text,
    interactive where possible).
18. **Plain language:** short sentences, bold-and-define new terms, consistent terms.

## Lesson recipe (order of sections)
1. Objectives (Bloom verbs) 2. Prerequisite check + refresher 3. Hook (Nigerian scenario)
4. Explore (3D practical / scene the student operates) 5. Explain (chunks, C→R→A)
6. Worked examples (2–3) 7. Guided practice (hints) 8. Misconceptions
9. Independent practice (exam-style, mixed Bloom) 10. Summary + recall quiz 11. Spaced review

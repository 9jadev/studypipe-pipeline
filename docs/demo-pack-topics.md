# Demo pack: 8 priority topics

Chosen because they are tested again and again in WAEC/NECO, schools lack the equipment to teach
them, and they are hard to picture from a textbook. Build #1 and #6 first: they make the strongest demos.
Confirm each topic against the current WAEC, NECO and JAMB syllabuses before building.

| # | slug | Subject | Topic | Must show visually |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ss2-chem-acid-base-titration | Chemistry | Acid–base titration | Burette/pipette handling, meniscus reading, end-point colour, results table → calculation |
| 2 | ss3-chem-qualitative-analysis | Chemistry | Tests for ions and gases | Precipitates forming and dissolving in excess, gas tests, Test/Observation/Inference table |
| 3 | ss2-phy-simple-pendulum | Physics | Simple pendulum (g) | Timing oscillations, table, T² vs l graph, slope → g (kit `pendulum` exists) |
| 4 | ss2-phy-refraction-glass-block | Physics | Refraction through a glass block | Pin placement and sighting, ray tracing, sin i vs sin r graph, apparent depth |
| 5 | ss2-phy-ohms-law-circuits | Physics | Ohm's law and circuits | Circuit building, meter connections, charge flow, V–I graph; metre bridge extension |
| 6 | ss1-bio-cell-structure | Biology | Plant and animal cells | **History shown as scenes** (Hooke → Leeuwenhoek → Schleiden & Schwann → Virchow), virtual microscope, 3D cells with working organelles |
| 7 | ss2-bio-heart-circulation | Biology | Heart and circulation | 3D heart sliced open, valves, double circulation, "follow a red blood cell" |
| 8 | ss2-math-circle-theorems | Mathematics | Circle theorems | Draggable points proving each theorem live, which-theorem-applies solver |

Every topic must also include: practical write-up guidance (tables, graphs, precautions, errors),
2–3 worked examples, misconceptions, and 12–20 original exam-style questions.

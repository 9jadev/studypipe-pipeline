### 1. `mass-spring` → v2 · `window.MassSpringKit` · file `kits/mass-spring/mass-spring.js` → `html/assets/js/mass-spring.js`
Used by: Hub (optional swap), L01 bench, L02 bench + zoom, L06 bench + zoom, L07 bench.
- **Geometry:** true helical spring (`CatmullRomCurve3` helix + `TubeGeometry`, rebuilt as pitch stretches; hook loops at both ends), clamp + boss on a retort stand, slotted-mass hanger with **extruded 50 g discs that have a real slot** (`THREE.Shape` with the slot cut), pointer on the hanger reading a vertical metre rule with a readable cm `CanvasTexture`, stopwatch with live display. Reference implementation: `html/assets/js/welcome-lab.js` (`helixGeometry`, `slottedDiscGeometry`, `buildSpringRig`).
- **opts / setState:** `mode` (`hooke | period | forces | energy | damping | testtube | okada`), `m` (kg, default 0.20), `k` (N m⁻¹, default 24.5), `A` (m), `medium` (`air | water | oil`), `view` (`bench | shock`), okada: `mass` (200 | 250 kg), `kOkada` 3.16e4, `zeta` (0.30 healthy | 0.05 leaking), `leak` (bool), `speed_kmh`, `d` 2.5, `Y` 0.010, `parked` (press-and-release, no ridges), `labels`.
- **Modes:**
  - `hooke`: add/remove 50 g → static extension x₀ = mg/k; `reading()` → `{ m, F, x_cm, pointer_cm }`.
  - `period`: pull & release, built-in stopwatch, `n` oscillations; `reading()` → `{ m, k, n, t, T, f, w, T2 }` with t = n·2π√(m/k) (no noise unless the caller adds reaction delay).
  - `forces`: restoring-force arrow ∝ x, always towards a dashed equilibrium plane; v and a arrows; counters `x, F = −kx, a = −(k/m)x`.
  - `energy`: KE / PE / total bars beside the spring (parts `ke-bar, pe-bar, total-bar`), E = ½kA².
  - `damping`: card vane on the hanger in a `LatheGeometry` beaker of water/oil (or air); amplitude × 0.97 / 0.75 / 0.40 per cycle; per-cycle amplitude list in `reading().amps`; period unchanged in air, ≈ +0.4 % in water.
  - `testtube`: weighted test-tube (lead shot visible) bobbing upright in a beaker of water; extra upthrust as restoring force.
  - `okada`: rear coil-over shock (coil spring `TubeGeometry` around a damper body; with `view: "shock"` a cut-away: piston with orifices moving through oil, oil jets that warm in colour, oil level low when `leak`). Body / road visuals come from `RidgeRoadProps.okada()` (load `ridge-road-props.js` first; fall back to the shock alone). Physics in `MassSpringKit.model.okada(speed_kmh, mass, zeta)` → `{ v, f, f0, r, A, th: g/(2πf)², lift }` using `A = Y√(1+(2ζr)²)/√((1−r²)²+(2ζr)²)`; amplitude builds with time constant 1/(ζω₀); crate is a free body (leaves when the carrier's downward acceleration > g; "crack" on landing faster than 0.12 m s⁻¹ relative) — see `welcome-lab.js` `stepRidge`. `parked: true`: press & release at f₀ (200 kg → T 0.50 s; 250 kg → 0.56 s; 20 bounces 10.0 s / 11.2 s).
- **Parts:** `spring, hanger, slotted-mass, mass, pointer, ruler, clamp, stand, stopwatch, vane, beaker, ke-bar, pe-bar, total-bar, testtube, rear-shock, piston, orifices, oil, carrier, crate, speedometer`.

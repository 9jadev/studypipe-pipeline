# Direction Script: Lesson 04 · The Beating Heart

## Overview
The video resolves Chidi's racing pulse and Baba Sule's two wrong ideas (the brain kicks every beat, and lub-dub is muscle clapping). It shows the 0.8 s cardiac cycle with valves, the cause of lub and dub, the SAN to Purkinje path, pulse and blood pressure, then works the lesson's four Apply examples (cycle time from a pulse count, cardiac output at rest and after the sprint, percentage rise, ventricle rest fraction) and reads Chidi's recovery curve. It opens the **Make Sense** phase, after the pulse bench. Practice questions stay in Apply.

**Accuracy notes:**
- Timings at 75 bpm as the lesson teaches: relaxed about 0.4 s, atria 0.1 s, ventricles 0.3 s, T = 0.8 s. Worked example 1 gives 0.83 s for 72 bpm, "close to the textbook 0.8 s".
- Lub = AV valves close as the ventricles start to squeeze; dub = semilunar valves close as they relax. The sounds are valves, not muscle.
- Cardiac output example uses the lesson's values (68 x 70, 132 x 95), in cm3 and dm3. The ratio 2.63 is said as "about 2.6".
- The recovery curve is drawn through the six bench readings (132, 116, 96, 80, 72, 68) as a smooth monotone curve levelling at 68; recovery about 5 min.
- Kit heart rate is 132 only in the hook and the last SAN shot; the heart kit shows the cycle at 75 bpm for the stages.
- Your-turn numbers (20 beats in 15 s, 80 bpm, T = 0.75 s) are fresh: they appear in no worked example, Do Now, exit ticket, MCQ or Part A/B item, so the Reflect exit ticket (21 beats) is not given away.
- Sphygmomanometer and 120/80 mmHg: first number ventricles contracting, second relaxed, as in the lesson.
